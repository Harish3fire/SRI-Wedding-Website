SRIVIDHYA & DIWAKAR WEDDING INVITATION
EDITING GUIDE

1. EDIT WEDDING CONTENT
Open src/main.jsx and edit the WEDDING object near the top.

2. LOCATION BUTTON
The venue is shown under LOCATION. The red button says:
GET DIRECTIONS TO MAHAL
Clicking it opens WEDDING.mapUrl.

3. EVENTS
Edit WEDDING.events to change Groom Welcoming, Reception, Engagement, Wedding/Muhurtham, or add more events.
Each event contains day, month, weekday, type, title and time.

4. VIDEO
Put your video at:
public/video/wedding.mp4
The video is displayed directly, without a poster/cover image.
Background music pauses when the video plays and resumes when it is paused or finished.

5. BACKGROUND MUSIC
Put your song at:
public/audio/wedding.mp3

6. IMAGES
Your local images belong in public/images/ and are referenced as /images/filename.jpg.

7. COUNTDOWN
WEDDING.countdownDate controls the countdown and fireworks trigger.
Current value: 30 October 2026, 8:00 AM IST.

8. GALLERY
The old 'Our Moments / A Little Gallery' section has been removed completely.

9. RUN
npm install
npm run dev
