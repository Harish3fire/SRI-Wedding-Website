YOUR WEDDING VIDEO

Put your own MP4 video in this folder with this exact filename:

wedding.mp4

The website currently expects:
/video/wedding.mp4

If you want a different filename or path, change WEDDING.video.src at the top of src/main.jsx.

Background music behaviour:
- When the wedding video starts, wedding.mp3 automatically pauses.
- When the video is paused, the background song resumes if it was playing before the video started.
- When the video finishes, the background song resumes if it was playing before the video started.
